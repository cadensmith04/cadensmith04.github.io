Head2Head: a trustless peer to peer betting contract

Bet your friend, not the book. Friendly bets have no enforcement and sportsbooks take a cut, so I wrote a contract that does neither.

  • Solo project
  • Solidity
  • Ethereum testnet demo

How it works

  • Create. Player A sets the matchup, the stake, the wallets allowed to accept, and a referee.
  • Escrow. Both players deposit their stake into the contract. Nobody, including the creator, can touch it until the bet settles.
  • Propose. Either player posts the result along with a bond.
  • Dispute. The other player has a set window to challenge. Lying costs you your bond.
  • Settle. With no dispute the result finalizes on its own and the winner withdraws the pot. With a dispute, the referee decides.
  • Refund. If the bet is never funded by the deadline, deposits come back.

Design choices

Each bet is its own small contract, so one bad bet can't affect another and gas stays low. The optimistic oracle means honest results settle with no referee at all, and the bond makes cheating a losing move.

The demo

In the video, Player A bets Player B 10 test tokens that the Falcons beat the Saints. Both fund, A proposes the result, B lets the dispute window pass, and A withdraws the 20 token pot.
