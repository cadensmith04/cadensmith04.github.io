MGT 3078, Spring 2025

Managing a $1M simulated portfolio

A semester running a simulated $1M portfolio, right as tariffs hit the market. We played defense and it held up.

  • Team of three
  • Equities, bonds, shorts
  • Jan to Apr 2025

Strategy

  • Growth from finance and electronic technology, about 68% of equity holdings, balanced with health care and consumer names.
  • Held as much as 69% cash during the uncertainty, then moved into bonds and defensive stocks as tariffs dragged on.
  • A few high beta positions for upside, and a short on Toyota on tariff exposure.

Results

  • Ended with a portfolio beta of 0.42 and a holding period return of −6.4%, about 10 points better than the S&P 500 over the same window.
  • Best trade: UnitedHealth, up 20.8%. Worst: a clean energy ETF bought as a tech rebound bet, sold for a $6,105 loss.
  • The Toyota short gained 4.9%.

What we'd change

Staying defensive protected us in the selloff but capped the upside. We'd move some cash and bonds into a wider set of low correlation holdings to keep risk low without sitting out the rebound.
