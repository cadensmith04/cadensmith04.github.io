1 THE TRAP

In 1985 Gilovich, Vallone and Tversky found players did not shoot better after makes, and the hot hand became the textbook example of seeing patterns in noise. In 2018 Miller and Sanjurjo showed the test itself was tilted: look inside a short sequence for what follows a streak, and a fair coin looks cold.

2 ROUND ONE: FIELD GOALS

Every NBA field goal attempt from 2015/16 through 2024/25, in game clock order within each player's game. The fix for the trap: shuffle each player's shots inside each game 400 times. That keeps every game's makes and misses but kills any streakiness, so the shuffled gap is what luck plus the bias produce. Real gap minus shuffled gap is the hot hand.

Plot twist: shot selection

After makes, players take harder shots. To check, every shot gets an expected make rate (xFG) from its distance, shot type, action type (dunk, pull up, step back and so on) and season. Then the same shuffle test runs on make minus xFG. Heaves over 40 feet are dropped.

Desk notes

STREAKS ARE SMALLThe measured streak effect is about a point, and after shot quality it is near zero. A player's base rate and tonight's role move a prop far more.
THE CLOSE ABSORBS ITTeams on streaks cover about half the time, and next week's spread already leans toward teams that just beat it. There is no streak edge left at the close.
THE RISK IS BELIEFThe money is in what bettors think. Streak chasing flow is one sided, which is an exposure and pricing question, not a forecasting one.

METHODS AND CAVEATSData sources, the exact tests, and what this cannot see.

Data. NBA shot charts and play by play from the shufinskiy/nba_data GitHub project (stats.nba.com sourced), 2015/16 to 2024/25. NFL play by play and schedules with closing lines from nflverse.

Streak test. For streak length k, compare the make rate right after k makes to the rate right after k misses, pooled over sequences. Streaks never cross games. The null comes from 400 within sequence shuffles (200 for the shot quality and no putback checks). Corrected effect is the real gap minus the average shuffled gap. When no shuffle matches the real value, p is reported as under 0.0025. Player bands are plus or minus 1.96 shuffle standard deviations; corrections use p values from that z score. Shrunk estimates use a normal empirical Bayes prior fit to all players in the view.

Why the shuffled gap is not zero. Two forces: the streak selection bias pulls it down, and good nights vs bad nights push it up because runs of makes cluster in a player's hot shooting games. The shuffle bakes in both.

Shot quality. xFG is the make rate in each cell of season, distance (1 foot bins), shot type and the 40 most common action types, shrunk toward the season, distance and shot type rate when a cell is thin. It ignores defenders, so it is a partial adjustment.

The line. A team covers when its margin beats the closing spread; pushes are dropped. Bands are binomial. Regressions are clustered by game.

Caveats. No defender distance, shot clock or tracking data. Shot order comes from the game clock. Putbacks are tagged by action type or a gap of 3 seconds or less. Free throw pairs use two shot trips only. NFL completions depend on play calls and coverage. No ticket or handle data, so bettor belief is assumed, not measured.
