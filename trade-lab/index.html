      How the numbers work

      • Roster strength is 65% this season's best lineup and 35% long term player value. Picks are ranked separately.
      • Adjusted totals use a 1.25 power sum, so two 5,000 players equal about 8,500.
      • Acceptance odds are a hand tuned model: market value they get, lineup and window fit, positional needs, their trade history habits, and how often they trade. A ranking signal, not a calibrated probability yet.
      • Scale: every source is rescaled to RosterAudit's scale, so RosterAudit numbers match its site.
      • Value at the time uses saved daily FantasyCalc snapshots when available, otherwise DynastyProcess weekly history (2021 on).
