      How the numbers work

      • Values come from FantasyCalc, built from real dynasty trades and refreshed daily. Win now uses their redraft value.
      • Adjusted totals use a 1.25 power sum, so two 5,000 players equal roughly 8,500, not 10,000.
      • Lineup impact re-solves each team's best starting lineup from your league's actual slots.
      • Window blends lineup strength rank with record and points scored.
      • Acceptance odds weigh the market value they receive, how much it helps their lineup and window, and how often they trade.
      • Next year's picks are priced early, mid, or late from projected finish.
