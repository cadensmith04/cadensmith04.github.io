CS 4641
Machine Learning
Spring 2026

Predicting market regimes with machine learning

A Gaussian Mixture Model labels each trading day since 2000 as steady growth, panic or rally from SPY, VIX and 10 year Treasury features. Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost then predict the next day's regime on a chronological split with 1,304 test days. XGBoost scored best, and rolling VIX volatility was the most important feature in both tree models.

Team of five. I built the models and cowrote the report.

Head2Head: a trustless peer to peer betting contract

A Solidity smart contract for private bets between friends. Both stakes sit in escrow, either player can propose the result by posting a bond, the other side has a window to dispute it, and a referee chosen up front settles disputes. Demoed live on a testnet with a Falcons and Saints bet.
