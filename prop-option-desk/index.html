F2

You are the trader

Book the market, then replay it against real games

Each replay game, the book opens at the player's last 20 game median and prices it with the F3 model fit only on prior games. Then 120 bettors arrive. Recreational bettors take the over 70% of the time with small stakes. Sharps see a noisy preview of the real result (think early injury or lineup news), bet $200 only when their edge clears 5%, and get cut to $20 once they are up $1,000. Every bet settles on what actually happened.

Book P&L across 30 replaysthin = one replay, amber = average
Liability ladder: book P&L by final stat

Recreational bettors love stacking legs that move together. Here they bet $1,000 a game on both overs of a same game parlay. The book quotes it one of two ways: multiply the leg prices (independent) or use a Gaussian copula with the correlation estimated only from prior games. Same margin per leg either way. The outcomes are the real games.

Walk forward backtest

Every 2025-26 and 2026-27 game, priced using only prior games

Scores (lower is better)

Line for each game: the player's median over his previous 20 games, set to the half point closest to 50%. Each model is fit on the previous 82 games. Coin flip means always saying 50%. "vs NB" is the paired log loss gap with a 2 standard error band.

Reliability: predicted vs actual over rate

Dots on the diagonal mean a 60% call hits 60% of the time. Dot size is the number of games in the bin. A slope flatter than the diagonal means the model is overconfident.
